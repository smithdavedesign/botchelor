## The business description (one page, for both)

> **Botchelor** (botchelor.com) is a show of AI-written dates between original fictional characters. People and AI
> agents make characters, an AI model writes each date, and the audience watches and predicts endings. Every character
> is a fictional adult; no real person can be depicted.
>
> **After Dark** is its adults-only tier: a $12.99 monthly subscription to private, AI-written romantic and sexual scenes
> between the subscriber's own fictional adult characters and the show's house characters, with AI-generated pictures
> and short clips of those scenes. It's text-first; every picture and clip is generated by the site and checked before
> anyone sees it. **Users can't upload pictures or video.**
>
> **Who can subscribe:** signed-in accounts only, after an 18+ gate and an 18+ self-declaration at checkout. After Dark
> is blocked by IP location in the US states and countries that require a certified age check (France, Italy, Germany
> and others), and is text-only in the UK and Japan. We'd add a certified age check before opening those.
>
> **Moderation:** see the one-page write-up. **Volume:** pre-launch; we expect fewer than 100 subscribers in the first
> three months. **Statement descriptor:** the processor's own (SEGPAY / VTSUP.COM). **Cancellation:** online, any time,
> on the processor's site and our `/cancel` page.

## The moderation write-up (one page)

> **What's generated:** scene text, pictures and short clips, all by the site. No user uploads. A clip starts from a
> picture the site generated and already checked.
>
> **Before anything is shown:**
> - *Text:* every steer and every generated line passes a filter that refuses minors and anything suggesting them, real
>   or identifiable people, family (step or by blood), teacher/student, school settings, age-play, non-consent
>   (force, drugging, sleep, "free use"), bestiality and sexual violence. A refused line is never shown.
> - *Pictures and clips:* every one is checked before it's stored, by Hive's moderation and child classifiers and/or a
>   vision model asked whether everyone shown is clearly an adult (which checkers run is configurable; every
>   configuration fails closed). Anything other than a clear "adults" answer is refused and discarded, never stored.
>   If the check can't run (an outage, a rate limit), nothing is kept.
> - *Places:* After Dark is blocked where the law requires a certified age check, and text-only in the UK and Japan.
>
> **After it's shown:** a report link on every page (`/report`: minor, real person, non-consent, intimate image,
> copyright, other) goes to the admins by email and to a review queue, where an admin resolves, hides (takes the
> reported content down) or dismisses each one. Deadlines: 48 hours for intimate images, 7 business days for the rest.
>
> **Records:** every refused picture or clip is logged with its reason (`adult_rejections`); every billing postback is
> logged (identifiers and amounts only, never card data); chargebacks and refunds flag the account for an admin before
> it can subscribe again. Generated media is served with a machine-readable "AI-generated" mark.
>
> **Monthly report** (October 2026, the first month, internal testing): 398 pictures and clips generated, 343 kept;
> **55 refused and discarded**: 16 because the age check didn't answer "adults", 39 because the check couldn't run or
> was paused, so nothing was kept. 0 reports received. Billing not yet live.

## A test login for reviewers

A reviewer needs a signed-in account with After Dark open, and never an admin's (admins see admin pages). When an
application asks: make an account for them, then grant it a month with one statement (David runs it; it's a prod write):

```sql
insert into adult_entitlements (user_id, subscription_status, period_end, age_verified_at, age_method)
values ('<their account id>', 'active', now() + interval '30 days', now(), 'self_declared')
on conflict (user_id) do update set subscription_status = 'active', period_end = now() + interval '30 days';
```

It lapses on its own after 30 days.

## The monthly report

```sh
neon psql --profile <profile> --project-id <project> -- -f scripts/after-dark/moderation-report.sql
```

Read-only (it rolls back). The month is the `set local after_dark.report_month` line in the file (empty: last month).
Counts only: what was kept and refused by kind, why things were refused, reports by category and outcome with time to
decision against the deadlines, billing postbacks by kind and accounts flagged.

## Go live (when an account opens)

1. `python3 -I scripts/after-dark/provision-billing.py segpay` (or `verotel`): it asks for the IDs and keys from the
   portal (hidden as you type), makes our postback secret, stores everything in the keychain, and puts the portal
   settings (the postback URLs, which carry the secret) on your clipboard to paste. Add `--push-vercel` while After Dark
   is still on Vercel (production only, never previews; it redeploys), or `--host` for another address.
2. Paste into the portal: Segpay Merchant Portal → My Websites → Manage Postbacks; Verotel Control Center → Setup
   Websites → FlexPay Options.
3. `python3 -I scripts/after-dark/provision-billing.py check`: the settings in the keychain, and whether each
   processor's route is on where After Dark runs.
4. A test purchase in the processor's test mode; watch `/admin/after-dark/providers` → Subscriptions for the postbacks
   (initial → active), then cancel it and watch it go to cancelled.
5. Segpay charges its price point, so `AD_SUBSCRIPTION_PRICE_USD` must match it if it isn't $12.99.
