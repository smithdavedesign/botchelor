# UX/UI audit, 2026-10-23

David asked, from his phone: "Audit the whole project's UX, desktop, mobile and After Dark. Make sure buttons align and things are neat and tidy. Where mobile is crowded, collapse into a hamburger-menu style. Characters have icons next to them in all places. No long scrolls."

**How:** `npm run e2e:visual` now covers every route, After Dark included (hub, a written scene, the theater, Shorts, Subscribe, settings), at desktop and phone, light and dark. That is 244 page states. Five reviewers read the screenshots against that brief, and a code sweep listed every character name shown without its face.

## Fixed

**Faces by every name**
- The live and After Dark theater headers.
- Episode agendas and confessionals, and watch-room goals.
- The swipe, matches, relationships and couple headings, plus the couple page's nudge, plans and "told each other" sections.
- Graph rows, buttons, search results and "In orbit".
- The directory's relationship-graph rows, the "Your matchmaking" rows and "Who it remembers".
- After Dark: the scene list, scene title, "Who's in", Shorts, Two sides, memory, the turn-ons switch and whispers.
- The /create send screen and its "Already have one?" links; originality look-alikes.
- Account agent characters and admin tables.
- A portrait that is still loading now shows the character's own gradient instead of a blank box.

**Phone crowding**
- The floating Feedback button covered form buttons, bubbles and headings on every page. On a phone it is now a row in the hamburger menu.
- The /create step rail no longer scrolls sideways. It says "Step N of 7 · name", and in edit mode the current step shows its number.
- After Dark: the steer bar was 360–400px. It is now one compact row (Nudge/Direct/🤫, input plus Write) with Rewind, Redo and Start over under "⋯ More". The theater's tools go under a "⋯" menu.
- The watch room's Vote and Chat sat ~2,900px down. They are now a bottom bar that opens a sheet.
- Admin analytics filters fold into a "Filters" disclosure.
- The footer's disclaimers are in "About this show". The 18+ line and crisis resources stay visible, and the links are a 2-column grid.

**Long scrolls**
- /agents: tools are one-line rows that open, groups are folded with counts, and the connect snippets are tabs.
- After Dark hub: pairs with lines come under "Continue", then a face picker; it shows 8 with "Show all".
- Scenarios show 4 then "Show N more"; the turn-ons show one character at a time.
- The send screen's house cast is compact rows: 6, then "Show all".
- Home shows 6 cast with "See every character →"; reunion shows the top 5 couples and 3 confessionals.
- Collapsed behind disclosures: the owner settings, the review form, the box-score "why" and the graph's caveats.
- The account key sections fold with a one-line status.
- Legal, privacy and terms get an "On this page" list.

**Alignment and tidiness**
- Cards lost the ~50px top gap on their first heading.
- Outline buttons are as tall as filled ones.
- These button rows no longer touch each other: Test/Save/Remove, Download/Sign out, and the admin sign-in forms.
- Tap targets of 24px or more for the footer feedback link, the looks dashes, the disclosure rows and the graph filter.
- After Dark settings scrolled sideways (1,704px wide); its labels now wrap.
- Fixed on the episode page:
  - the duplicate finale paragraph;
  - the duplicate bonus-question line;
  - "Mira Quell Mira Quell wants…";
  - the title and kicker wrapping.
- League ranks show "–" at 0 points.
- Equal-height scenario and cast cards.
- In the wizard:
  - the review step is grouped with an Edit link per group;
  - the edit action row and phone save bar;
  - the "Drawn to" legend;
  - the Add button height;
  - one style for selected pills.
- The originality note shows words as written ("flinches", not "flinche"); /terms no longer shows privacy wording; the keys page has no "deployment'sSESSION_SECRET".

**The audit itself**
- Screenshots now scroll first so lazy faces load.
- They are taken before every `<details>` is opened, so they show the page as a reader sees it. The audit still opens them afterwards to check the fields inside.
- The ship and subscribe routes now go to real pages.
- The After Dark stub writes new lines on every step.
- Image decoding has a 3-second cap; it used to hang forever on a lazy image.

## Not done (flagged)

- **`npm run e2e` fails 7 of 34 steps on main too.** The steps are the landing builder, matchup avatars, the premiere room and theater, the template room, and the /cast heading. Before this branch, `main` failed the same 7, so the script has drifted from the site (CI is manual-only). It needs its own pass.
- The episode page's agendas, confessionals and dealbreaker could merge into one "Behind the scenes" card.
- Notification rows have no faces: they carry only a title the server wrote, with no character ids.
- Thread preview on a phone: your own message loses its right alignment and face (`ThreadPreview.tsx`). A proper own-face slot in the messages header needs `TextPhone.tsx`; for now it is placed with CSS `order`.
- Full-page phone screenshots taller than 16,384 device px repeat the top of the page (a Chromium limit). Judge the bottom of such pages from the desktop shot.
