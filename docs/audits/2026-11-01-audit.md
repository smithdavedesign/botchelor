# Botchelor audit, 2026-11-01: reliability, accessibility and SEO, first-visitor journeys

The second audit after the 2026-10-31 UX audit (`docs/audits/2026-10-31-ux-audit.md`, now done except After Dark's
pages). It looks at what that one didn't: what can take the site down or make it slow, who can't use it, how it looks to
search and link previews, and what a first-time visitor actually goes through. After Dark is left out (botchelor-4a's).

How it was run, all on botchelor.com against `f54e8cb`, in a real browser (Chromium, phone 390×844 and desktop
1280×800), one browser at a time. Health was watched every 30 s throughout and answered 200 every time.

- **Reliability and speed (R):** 37 pages measured (requests, bytes, time to first byte, prefetches), DigitalOcean run
  logs and app spec (read-only), and the code behind the slow paths.
- **Accessibility, SEO and links (X):** axe-core 4.14 on 36 pages × phone/desktop × light/dark, keyboard walks of the
  main flows and every modal, every page's metadata and share image, crawler user agents past the 18+ gate, and a
  300-URL link crawl.
- **First-visitor journeys (J):** six journeys as a brand-new visitor: cold landings, making a character through to the
  first aired date, what brings them back, an account, running out of dates, things going wrong. One test character
  and two dates were made on production; see *Test data* below.

## The short version

1. **This morning's outages have one root cause, and it's still there:** every request on the one replica, the health
   check included, shared **3 database connections that wait forever** (R1). Full-size pictures and clips are read out
   of Postgres on every request (R2) and Cloudflare caches none of them (R3), so any page that asks for many pictures
   queues the whole site. #1185 made /graph ask for thumbnails; the queue was still 12–22 s deep on /graph today.
   **Hotfix #1217:** 10 connections with a 10 s wait limit, health on its own connection, and the worst full-size
   reads gone.
2. **Two redirects point at `https://localhost:8080`** (R8): every house cast portrait served through `/api/assets`
   (cached a day, so they show blank on /graph), and the 18+ gate for anyone who taps before the page's script loads.
   **Fixed in #1217.**
3. **A first date lost its dialogue** in front of the visitor (J1): six lines streamed live, then the episode was saved
   without a transcript and the page fell back to a prose recap. The first episode is the product's one promise.
4. **Nothing brings an anonymous visitor back** (J3, J4): matches, agreed dates and texts happen within minutes, but
   the only notification opt-in is behind ⚙ on /notifications, and a returning visitor gets the first-visit home page.
5. **Accessibility is mostly sound** (dark-mode contrast, alt text, link health, crawler access all clean) but every
   bottom sheet drops focus to the page top when it closes (X1), and the showcase viewer and swipe deck don't work for
   keyboard and screen-reader users (X2, X4).
6. **Character pages share as "Botchelor"**, with a card image that takes 8–11 s and isn't cached (X3, R7).

## The biggest wins, in order

1. **Stop the site falling over** (R1, R8, R9 done in #1217; then R3 the Cloudflare rule, R2 the full-size reads,
   R4 a liveness check and alerts, R5 prefetching, R7 share images). Nothing else matters if a shared link takes the
   replica down.
2. **Never lose the first date's lines** (J1).
3. **Ask to notify at the peak, and greet returning visitors with what happened** (J3, J4): the loop already runs on
   its own within minutes; visitors just never hear about it.
4. **A face for every character** (J2) and **a shorter path to the first episode** (J7, J23).
5. **Sheets give focus back, and character pages share as themselves** (X1, X3): one fix each in a shared component.
