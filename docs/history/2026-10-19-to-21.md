## 5. The production outage (2026-10-20, ~14:30 to ~22:40 PDT, about 8 hours)

**Symptom:** every database call failed with Neon error 53000 ("Your account or project has exceeded the quota"). `/api/health` returned 503, so every page that reads the database failed.

**Root cause:** the Neon project used **5.94 GB of the free plan's 5 GB of monthly network transfer**, and Neon suspends compute until the next month when that happens. Compute (32 of 100 CU-hours) and storage (183 MB of 1 GB) were fine. The first diagnosis, "too much data stored", was wrong: going over the storage cap only blocks writes, it doesn't suspend the database.

Where the transfer went:
- **After Dark media served from Postgres with `cache-control: no-store`.** Videos are ~10 MB each, and the reel player preloaded every shot on each page load. Every view, replay and start-frame fetch re-read the full bytes. This was the main cause.
- **The nightly `pg_dump` backup:** ~150–180 MB a night, about 1 GB a week.
- **The busy days of loops and simulations** (MCP calls, dates, polling).
- The database grew from 4 MB to ~150 MB in two days once After Dark media went in.

**Why it took a while to find:**
- The database is owned by the Neon account **1426davejobs.agent@gmail.com**, which was set up by hand on 2026-10-13.
- The Neon tools in the Claude sessions, and the local CLI, were signed into a different account (**1426dave@gmail.com**, org "Smith Dave Design").
- It isn't a Vercel Marketplace integration, and no Neon emails go to 1426davejobs@gmail.com.
- David found the right account in the end.

**Fix:**
1. David upgraded that Neon org to **Launch** (pay for what you use: 500 GB transfer included, $0.106 per CU-hour) and set a **$20 spending notification**. The site was back within a minute or two, with no data lost.
2. **#499** (merged and deployed): the admin's browser keeps a finished After Dark file (`private, max-age=31536000, immutable`) instead of downloading it again.
3. In progress: move media bytes out of Postgres into object storage (see "In progress" below).
