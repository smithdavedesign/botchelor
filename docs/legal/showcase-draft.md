# Draft for counsel: the creator showcase (not live)

**Status:** a draft for counsel, not shipped. The live legal page (`app/legal/sections.tsx`) is unchanged.

## What would change

Today the legal page says After Dark's pictures, videos and voiced lines are "never shown on the main show unless you
send it there yourself". The showcase is a way of sending them there: one item at a time, by its owner. Proposed
wording:

> **The showcase.** You can publish a picture or clip you made to the showcase, under a creator handle you choose.
> Nothing is published unless you publish it, one item at a time, and you can unpublish it at any time. A picture
> that shows someone else's character goes up only once its owner says yes, and either of you can take it down.
> Explicit work on the showcase is shown only to signed-in, age-verified people where After Dark is open. Everything
> on the showcase is made with AI, and every character is a fictional adult. Each post says how it was made: the AI
> model, who's in it and, unless you choose not to share it, the prompt. Report anything that breaks the rules, and
> we'll take it down.

## Questions for counsel

1. Does publishing explicit AI-generated work of fictional adults to other age-verified subscribers change our
   obligations? Consider record-keeping, 2257-style requirements, and payment-processor rules for user-published
   adult content (Segpay or Verotel).
2. Is per-item opt-in, plus the other owner's consent for pairs, enough consent for characters made by two people?
3. What takedown process and response time should we commit to?
4. Should the explicit showcase be geo-limited beyond After Dark's own jurisdiction rules?
5. A post shows the prompt that made it unless its creator unticks that (2026-10-29). Is a creator's prompt their
   personal data or their work, for privacy and ownership? And does showing the model names need anything from the
   model providers' terms?
